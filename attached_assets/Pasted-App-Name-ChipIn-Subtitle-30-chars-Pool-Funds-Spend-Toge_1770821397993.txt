App Name: ChipIn

Subtitle (30 chars): Pool Funds. Spend Together.

Description:

ChipIn makes group money simple. Whether you're splitting a trip, collecting for a gift, or pooling funds with friends for a big purchase — ChipIn handles it all in one app.

Create a pool, invite your crew, and watch contributions roll in. When you're ready to spend, use your virtual Visa card linked directly to your pool — online or in-store.

KEY FEATURES

Pool Funds Together
Set up pools for trips, events, gifts, or anything else. Share a link, and anyone can chip in. Track progress with real-time updates and hit your goal faster.

Virtual Visa Cards
Get a virtual debit card tied to your pool. Use it anywhere Visa is accepted — online shopping, subscriptions, or tap-to-pay in stores.

Pay Me Back Links
Send a simple link to collect money from anyone — no sign-up required for them.

Spend Now Marketplace
Browse curated partner stores and shop directly with your pool funds. Exclusive discounts from partnered businesses.

Wallet & Bank Transfers
Deposit funds, track your balance, and withdraw to your bank account whenever you want.

Smart Auto-Contributions
Set up recurring contributions so you never forget to chip in. Weekly, biweekly, or monthly — you choose.

Secure by Design
Identity verification (KYC), two-factor authentication, and encrypted transactions keep your money safe.

Real-Time Notifications
Get instant alerts when someone contributes, when your pool hits a milestone, or when your card is used.

Rewards & Badges
Earn points and unlock badges as you contribute and create pools. Climb the leaderboard and show off your ChipIn status.

Download ChipIn today and take the hassle out of group money.
